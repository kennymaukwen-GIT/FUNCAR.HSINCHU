How buying a car from us works

  1. Tell us what you need. Budget, size, brand, or a specific car from the list above.
  2. See and drive the car at our Hsinchu showroom. We show you the Goo inspection certificate.
  3. Sign and pay. We go through the contract with you in English.
  4. We do the paperwork. Registration transfer into your name at the motor vehicles office, licence plates and compulsory insurance. You provide your ARC (a Gold Card counts as an ARC) and a seal or signature.
  5. Drive away. Servicing and repairs stay with the same shop after you buy.

Want a car that is not in stock? We import cars to order from overseas markets. Delivery typically takes 6 to 10 weeks, depending on shipping, customs and the mandatory safety and emissions inspection.

New to driving in Taiwan? Read our guide: Buying a car in Taiwan as a foreigner, covering documents, driver's licences, insurance and yearly costs.

Leaving Taiwan? Sell your car to us

We buy used cars, especially MINI, Mercedes-Benz, BMW and Porsche. We give you a price based on the car's condition and the current market, and we can come to see the car.

Plan the sale before your ARC expires. Taiwan's motor vehicles offices need a valid residence permit to transfer a car, and any unpaid licence plate tax, road maintenance fee or traffic fines must be paid before the car can change owner. If you have already left Taiwan, the transfer needs a power of attorney authenticated by a Taiwan mission abroad, which takes time. Selling a few weeks before you leave is the simplest option.

Yes. With an Alien Resident Certificate (ARC) or Gold Card you can buy a car and register it in your own name. FUN CAR in Hsinchu handles the registration transfer, licence plates and compulsory insurance for cars bought from us.
